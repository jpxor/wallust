Secrets

Your secrets. Your backup.

Secrets is an open-source Android app for storing passwords, usernames and tokens in an encrypted vault. Unlock with a master passphrase or optional fingerprint authentication.

Use it offline, export an encrypted backup, or back up to your Google Drive account or an SFTP server you control. Google Drive access uses only the drive.appdata scope to store and restore encrypted backups in Secrets' private app folder; it does not grant access to your other Drive files. The maintainer receives no copy of your vault or Google account tokens.
